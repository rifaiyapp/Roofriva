# ROOFRIVA Template Guide

Update `src/data/site.ts` first for business name, phone, email, hours, service area and service definitions. Replace demo imagery with verified client photography where available. Review every service claim, warranty, license, insurance statement and review before publishing.

## Go-live checklist
- Replace demo brand/contact details and logo
- Add verified address/service areas and hours
- Replace sample testimonials with verified reviews
- Replace demo imagery with client-approved assets where available
- Verify service descriptions and real process
- Confirm form delivery and recipient
- Configure analytics/call tracking only if approved
- Review Privacy/Terms/Accessibility with the client
- Replace canonical domain
- Remove `noindex,nofollow` and update `robots.txt`
- Add verified business schema only after real data is supplied
- Test forms, calls, links, sitemap, mobile, accessibility and Core Web Vitals
